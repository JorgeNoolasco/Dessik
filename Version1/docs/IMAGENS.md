# Ilustrações da loja

Arquivo: `public/images/catalogo-dessik.png`.

Criado com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen, sem API/CLI externo. Ilustrações de produtos fictícios, sem marcas reais. Um atlas de quatro colunas por duas linhas é recortado visualmente pelo CSS; a imagem original permanece no projeto.

Prompt utilizado:

> Use case: product-mockup. Create ONE product catalog sprite sheet image, landscape 2:1 ratio, with precisely FOUR columns and TWO rows of EIGHT equally sized SQUARE cells, edge-to-edge, no gutters or dividing lines. Every cell has same very pale cool gray #f2f4f8 seamless studio background. Each object is entirely contained and centered inside its own cell, occupying 65 percent of the cell with generous margin. Premium photorealistic 3D studio render of fictional unbranded technology gadgets, silver and matte black, subtle cobalt blue accents and realistic soft shadows. Exact cell order left to right: TOP ROW: open silver laptop with blue abstract screen, black wireless gaming mouse, white compact mechanical keyboard, thin desktop monitor with blue abstract screen and silver stand. BOTTOM ROW: black over-ear headset with microphone, small black USB webcam with clip stand, dark silver 2.5 inch SSD drive, two dark RAM memory modules. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no UI or labels. Clean consistent luxury ecommerce product imagery. Objects must not cross cell boundaries. This is a single coherent contact-sheet asset used as a CSS sprite.

Produtos já cadastrados com o placeholder antigo recebem a nova ilustração na vitrine pelo nome, sem alterar preço ou estoque no banco. URLs de imagens personalizadas são preservadas.
